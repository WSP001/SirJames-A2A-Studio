## Canon Locks

This is a Sir James Adventures book, not a replacement for Book001 or Book002.

- Sir James remains a 4-6 year old knight-in-training.
- Claude remains a purebred Redbone Coonhound with thought bubbles, not spoken dialogue.
- Gramps remains the wise retired knight mentor.
- Sparky remains the comic relief squirrel.
- King Arthur may appear as quest giver when needed.
- Finnian the Fox and Droggo the Dragon may appear because they already exist in legacy story trees.
- Sir James does not wear heavy armor or a helmet.
- Sir James uses a wooden practice sword, not sharp steel weapons.
- The story keeps the virtue system: courage, wisdom, trust, kindness, empathy, patience, and perseverance.
- The story supports the Parent Dashboard as the "Room of Many Windows."
- Book003 carries Book002 patterns forward: 10 chapters, 8 scenes per chapter, narration batches, image prompts, audio-ready dialogue, and parent/child reflection.

## Book Promise

Sir James learns that the best adventures do not only happen in forests, caves, bridges, and castles. Some adventures happen when a child learns how to listen, choose, read, ask for help, try again, and share what he knows.

The first half of the book builds survival skills. The second half turns those skills into reading skills. The Room of Many Windows lets parents quietly guide the adventure without taking the wonder away from the child.

## Prologue: The Room of Many Windows

Gramps leads Sir James, Claude, and Sparky into a round stone room hidden beneath the old puzzle-stone house. The walls are full of glowing windows. Some show forests. Some show caves. Some show rivers, mountains, stars, and small words that shimmer like fireflies.

"What is this place?" Sir James asks.

Gramps taps his staff on the floor. "This is the Room of Many Windows. Every window opens when a grown-up chooses how to help a child learn."

Claude sits close beside Sir James. A thought bubble appears above his head: *We are not alone. The helpers are watching kindly.*

Sparky hops onto a windowsill and nearly tumbles into a glowing picture of a river. "Do the windows have snacks?"

Gramps smiles. "No snacks. But they do have choices."

The first window brightens. It shows a quiet green wood.

"Then I choose adventure," Sir James says.

The floor glows with three familiar marks: courage, wisdom, and trust.

Book003 begins.

## Chapter 1: Whispering Woods

**Virtue focus:** Wisdom and emotional safety  
**Learning focus:** Listening, safety, asking for help  

### Scene 1: Cottage Morning
Sir James wakes at Gramps's cottage and finds his blue tunic folded beside a small wooden shield. Claude waits by the door. Sparky taps the window as if the day is late.

Choice: Take the sunny path with Gramps's map, or rush toward the shadowy shortcut.

### Scene 2: The First Whisper
At the edge of the woods, the trees whisper too softly to understand unless Sir James stops walking. Claude's thought bubble reminds him: *Brave can be quiet first.*

Choice: Slow down and listen, or guess and keep moving.

### Scene 3: The Mossy Guide
A moss-covered forest guide appears beside a bent oak and offers either a riddle or a clear hint. Gramps lets Sir James choose the kind of help he needs.

Choice: Try the riddle, or ask for the hint.

### Scene 4: The Hollow Log Boom
A hollow log booms in the wind. Sparky hides behind Sir James's shoulder, then pretends he was "guarding from the back." Sir James admits he felt scared.

Choice: Take three calm breaths with Claude, or make a silly courage song with Sparky.

### Scene 5: The Safe Ring
The group finds a circle of smooth stones where the forest feels peaceful. Sir James marks the map with a tiny star.

Choice: Mark where help was needed, or mark only where he was brave.

### Scene 6: Gramps's Question
Gramps asks, "Which helped you more today, bravery or wisdom?" Sir James realizes they worked together.

Choice: Choose courage, wisdom, or trust for the chapter badge.

### Scene 7: Claude's Bubble
Claude's thought bubble glows: *You listened before you leaped. That is knight work.*

Choice: Save Claude's thought as a reminder, or ask Gramps for a new reminder.

### Scene 8: Window Opens
Back in the Room of Many Windows, the forest window becomes a small glowing badge. Another window opens to crystals underground.

Reflection prompt: "When did slowing down help Sir James?"

## Chapter 2: Crystal Caves

**Virtue focus:** Curiosity and patience  
**Learning focus:** Patterns, questions, and hints  

### Scene 1: The Shimmering Entrance
The Crystal Caves open only when Sir James notices the color pattern on the door: blue, green, blue, green.

Choice: Match colors, match shapes, or ask Claude for a hint.

### Scene 2: Echo Questions
Every question Sir James asks echoes back in a different voice. "What do I notice?" becomes "What did I almost miss?"

Choice: Ask about what he sees, what he feels, or what he can try next.

### Scene 3: Claude's Crystal Path
Claude sniffs a safe path across glowing stepping stones. A bubble appears: *Try the pattern before the leap.*

Choice: Step by color order, size order, or sound order.

### Scene 4: Sparky's Wrong Turn
Sparky races ahead and bumps into a crystal that plays his own giggle back to him three times. He bows like he meant to do it.

Choice: Laugh and reset, or gently remind Sparky to wait.

### Scene 5: The Dark Pocket
The cave grows quiet. Sir James cannot see the next step. He puts one hand on Claude's collar and one hand on the cave wall.

Choice: Sing softly, hold still and breathe, or call for Gramps.

### Scene 6: The Pattern Door
A door asks for one final pattern. Sir James must choose the missing crystal.

Choice: Red, blue, red, what comes next?

### Scene 7: The Crystal Ledger
The cave records patterns solved, hints used, and questions asked. Gramps says the ledger is not for judging. It is for learning.

Choice: Save the easiest clue or the hardest clue.

### Scene 8: Window Opens
The Room of Many Windows shows the cave badge beside the forest badge. A bridge glows in the next window.

Reflection prompt: "What question helped Sir James most?"

## Chapter 3: The Labyrinth and the Bridge of Light

**Virtue focus:** Trust and teamwork  
**Learning focus:** Strategy, turn-taking, shared choices  

### Scene 1: The Labyrinth Gate
Sir James finds a gate made of living branches. The Labyrinth Guardian watches with green glowing eyes.

Choice: Enter alone to prove courage, or enter with Claude and Sparky.

### Scene 2: The Three Turns
The path splits three ways. Sparky wants the shiny way. Claude points his nose toward the quiet way. Gramps asks Sir James to explain his thinking.

Choice: Follow shine, scent, or map.

### Scene 3: The Lost Bell
A small bell rings somewhere inside the maze. Sir James must listen and move one careful step at a time.

Choice: Rush toward the sound, or count steps together.

### Scene 4: The Guardian's Test
The Guardian asks, "What is stronger than one brave knight?" Sir James looks at his friends.

Choice: Answer "a sword," "a plan," or "a team."

### Scene 5: The Bridge Appears
A golden bridge of light stretches across a misty chasm, but it only grows solid when everyone steps together.

Choice: Let Gramps lead, let Sir James lead, or count together.

### Scene 6: Claude's Proud Thought
Claude sits tall. His bubble says: *We did it together, brave Sir James. I am proud of you.*

Choice: Thank Claude, thank everyone, or ask the Guardian what comes next.

### Scene 7: The Guardian's Approval
The Guardian raises one branch-arm toward the shining bridge. The mist below turns into rainbow prisms.

Choice: Cross now, or pause to help Sparky stop shaking.

### Scene 8: Window Opens
The bridge badge joins the others. The next window shows smoky mountains and a dragon's tail.

Reflection prompt: "Why was teamwork stronger than rushing?"

## Chapter 4: Droggo and the Sore Tail

**Virtue focus:** Empathy and courage  
**Learning focus:** Seeing past first impressions  

### Scene 1: The Roar in the Pass
A roar shakes the Misty Mountains. Sparky whispers, "Do we run now or later?" Claude's bubble answers: *Big creatures hurt too.*

Choice: Approach gently, or hide and observe.

### Scene 2: The Thorn
Sir James sees a thorn stuck in Droggo's tail. The dragon is not angry first. He is hurting first.

Choice: Offer help, or ask Gramps to speak first.

### Scene 3: Gentle Hands
Sir James removes the thorn while Claude stays calm and Gramps speaks softly.

Choice: Tell Droggo why kindness matters, or let silence be kind.

### Scene 4: Droggo's Question
Droggo asks, "Why would a knight help a dragon?" Sir James answers, "Because hurt is hurt, even when it roars."

Choice: Invite Droggo to be a friend, or thank him and give him space.

### Scene 5: Sparky's Apology
Sparky admits he called Droggo "too big and smoky." Droggo snorts one tiny puff of smoke and accepts the apology.

Choice: Practice apology words, or practice forgiveness words.

### Scene 6: The Warm Stone
Droggo warms a stone path so the friends can cross safely.

Choice: Ride Droggo across, or walk beside him.

### Scene 7: The Dragon Bow
Droggo bows, and the mountain mist clears.

Choice: Earn empathy, courage, or kindness badge.

### Scene 8: Window Opens
The dragon window closes gently, not with a roar but with a sigh.

Reflection prompt: "How did Sir James know Droggo needed help?"

## Chapter 5: The Clockwork Library

**Virtue focus:** Perseverance and memory  
**Learning focus:** Practice, recall, and transfer  

### Scene 1: The Library Door
The Clockwork Library ticks and hums. Books slide across shelves like tiny trains.

Choice: Choose the book with pictures, words, or sounds.

### Scene 2: The Forgetful Shelf
A shelf forgets where it put the map. Sir James must remember the order of three clues.

Choice: Repeat aloud, draw it, or ask Claude's bubble to hold one clue.

### Scene 3: Sparky's Page Storm
Sparky sneezes and sends pages flying. The words scatter into the air.

Choice: Catch the biggest pages first, or sort by matching symbols.

### Scene 4: Gramps's Memory Trick
Gramps teaches Sir James to connect a new clue to something he already knows.

Choice: Link the clue to courage, wisdom, or trust.

### Scene 5: The Practice Door
A clockwork door only opens after trying again without getting angry.

Choice: Try once more, ask for help, or take a calm pause.

### Scene 6: The Transfer Table
The library shows how forest listening, cave patterns, bridge teamwork, and dragon empathy all help with reading.

Choice: Pick the skill that helped most.

### Scene 7: The Librarian Gear
A tiny brass gear stamps Sir James's map: "Ready for word quests."

Choice: Keep practicing, or open the river window.

### Scene 8: Bridge to Reading
The Room of Many Windows shifts. The next window shows a river made of words.

Reflection prompt: "What did Sir James learn that he can use again?"

## Chapter 6: The River of Choices

**Virtue focus:** Confidence and direction  
**Learning focus:** Word families and direction words  

### Scene 1: Fork in the River
Sir James sees signs that read "up," "down," "left," and "right." Claude noses the "up" sign.

Choice: Read the sign, follow the picture, or ask for narration.

### Scene 2: Rock-Step Word Hops
Stones across the river show word families: cat, bat, hat, dog, log, fog.

Choice: Hop by rhyme, hop by picture, or hop with Claude.

### Scene 3: Sparky's Word Raft
Sparky builds a raft but labels the front "back." Everyone pauses.

Choice: Fix the label kindly, or test which way the raft moves.

### Scene 4: Calm Cove
Short sentences float by: "The dog is in the boat." "We go up the river."

Choice: Tap words while narrator reads, or try reading one line alone.

### Scene 5: Foggy Word Bend
Fog covers the pictures, leaving only words.

Choice: Use sound, shape, or memory.

### Scene 6: Claude's Bark Clue
Claude barks once for the word "dog" and wags for the word "log." Sir James laughs.

Choice: Sort dog/log/fog words.

### Scene 7: The Rapids Review
The river speeds up, but Sir James does not panic. He reads one word at a time.

Choice: Earn word badge or direction badge.

### Scene 8: Window Opens
The word river flows into a mountain echo.

Reflection prompt: "Which word did Sir James read with the most confidence?"

## Chapter 7: Echo Mountain

**Virtue focus:** Patience and practice  
**Learning focus:** Blends and spelling  

### Scene 1: Base Camp Echo
Echo Mountain repeats words back in pieces: cl-ap, st-op, tr-ee.

Choice: Clap the blend, tap the blend, or say the whole word.

### Scene 2: Misty Signposts
Signs point toward cliff, cloud, stone, trail, and bridge.

Choice: Choose the sign that starts with "cl," "st," or "tr."

### Scene 3: Letter Tiles Cave
Loose letter tiles slide around like ice.

Choice: Build "stop," "trip," or "clap."

### Scene 4: Sparky's Echo Joke
Sparky shouts "snack!" and the mountain echoes "back!" He declares this educational.

Choice: Find words that rhyme, or words that start alike.

### Scene 5: Gramps's Fire Story
By a small fire, Gramps tells a story where key words glow when Sir James reads them.

Choice: Tap brave, trail, stop, and clap.

### Scene 6: The Slippery Slope
Sir James spells too fast and mixes letters. Claude's bubble says: *Slow letters make strong words.*

Choice: Try slower, ask for first two letters, or listen once more.

### Scene 7: Summit Echo
At the summit, Sir James reads a full phrase: "Stay kind. Check again."

Choice: Choose the phrase that sounds most like knight wisdom.

### Scene 8: Window Opens
The mountain echo becomes a city gate.

Reflection prompt: "What helped Sir James when spelling got hard?"

## Chapter 8: The City of Many Doors

**Virtue focus:** Responsibility and shared choices  
**Learning focus:** Functional reading and short sentences  

### Scene 1: Gatehouse Signs
The city gate has labels: open, shut, in, out. Sir James must read before entering.

Choice: Tap "open" or "in."

### Scene 2: Stop, Look, Go
A busy crossing teaches Sir James to put sentence steps in order.

Choice: Stop first, look first, or go first.

### Scene 3: Claude's Delivery
Claude carries a tiny message: "Go up the stairs." Sir James must match the sentence to the right picture.

Choice: Up, down, left, or right.

### Scene 4: Sparky's Shortcut
Sparky finds a fast road, a safe road, a bumpy road, and a quiet road.

Choice: Pick by adjective.

### Scene 5: Finnian's Door Test
Finnian bows beside three doors. "Clever, quick, or kind?" he asks.

Choice: Open the clever door alone, or the kind door with friends.

### Scene 6: The Shared Key
The key only turns when Sir James lets the group choose together.

Choice: Vote, take turns, or ask Gramps.

### Scene 7: City Passport
The city stamps Sir James's reading passport with direction words and sentence steps.

Choice: Review easiest word or hardest word.

### Scene 8: Window Opens
The city lights dim, and stars bloom above an orchard.

Reflection prompt: "When was reading useful in the city?"

## Chapter 9: Star Orchard

**Virtue focus:** Wonder and expression  
**Learning focus:** Adjectives and description  

### Scene 1: Choosing a Star Tree
Star trees grow bright, soft, tall, small, quiet, and busy.

Choice: Match adjective to tree.

### Scene 2: Tasting Notes
A fallen star-fruit is warm and sweet. Another is cold and bright.

Choice: Complete "It is ___ and ___."

### Scene 3: Claude's Preferences
Claude's bubble says: *I like the quiet star.* Sir James reads the sentence and points to it.

Choice: Build "I like..." or "I do not like..."

### Scene 4: Sparky's Sorting Game
Sparky sorts hot stars, cold stars, heavy stars, and light stars into baskets.

Choice: Sort by word, not picture.

### Scene 5: Gramps's Description Lesson
Gramps says descriptions are lanterns for the imagination.

Choice: Pick two words to describe Sir James.

### Scene 6: Finnian's Almost Trick
Finnian labels a tiny star "huge" and waits to see if Sir James notices.

Choice: Correct kindly, laugh first, or ask why.

### Scene 7: Night-Sky Poem
Sir James builds a short poem from words he has learned: brave, kind, bright, soft, sky.

Choice: Choose the final poem word.

### Scene 8: Window Opens
The orchard turns into a home path under starlight.

Reflection prompt: "Which words made the best picture in your mind?"

## Chapter 10: Homecoming Circle

**Virtue focus:** Reflection and perseverance  
**Learning focus:** Review and transfer  

### Scene 1: Path of Remembering
The path home shows icons from every chapter. Sir James reads, remembers, and smiles.

Choice: Start with forest, cave, bridge, dragon, or library memory.

### Scene 2: Campfire Word Stories
At the fire, Sir James uses words like because, and, brave, kind, and trust to tell what happened.

Choice: Complete a sentence about one chapter.

### Scene 3: Home Desk Spelling
Gramps sets out letter tiles, not as a test to fear but as a game to see growth.

Choice: Spell a strong word, a kind word, or a bright word.

### Scene 4: Claude's Quiet Pride
Claude rests his head on Sir James's knee. His bubble says: *You tried again. That is how knights grow.*

Choice: Save Claude's thought or share it with Gramps.

### Scene 5: Sparky's Celebration
Sparky tries to hang every badge at once and falls into the soft laundry basket.

Choice: Laugh, help him out, or make a badge wall together.

### Scene 6: The Parent Window
The Room of Many Windows shows the grown-up view: virtues earned, words practiced, hints used, and favorite story paths.

Choice: Choose more feelings adventures, more puzzle adventures, or a mix.

### Scene 7: King Arthur's Message
A royal window opens. King Arthur says Sir James has not become a knight because he never needed help. He is becoming a knight because he knows how to learn.

Choice: Accept the Squire of the Many Windows badge.

### Scene 8: Book004 Seed
One last window opens to the horizon: sky, sea, road, and starlight. Gramps asks, "Where shall we learn next?"

Final reflection: "What should Sir James explore in the next adventure?"
