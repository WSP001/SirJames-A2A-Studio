## 🎬 OPENING: Sir James as Our Test Pilot

### The Room of Many Windows

Sir James stands in a round stone hall called the **Room of Many Windows**, where each glowing window secretly represents a parent's dashboard screen in our world.

Gramps explains that every time a grown-up:
- **Taps a button** → The windows shimmer
- **Types in the chat box** → New clues appear
- **Changes a slider** → Adventures adjust

Sir James isn't just the hero—he's the **first explorer** of this new "Parents' Control Tower," bravely trying each challenge so:
- Children can have **fun while learning**
- Story Guides (A2A agents) can **watch the signals**
- The **Book of Measurements** captures what works

> **Meta-Purpose:** The real 5-year-old tester (Sir James Sr.'s grandson) IS Sir James, experiencing each interactive challenge as the character himself tests the deployment.
