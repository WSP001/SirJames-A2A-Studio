The Promise

📖 Narrator: Sir James stood up straight and placed his hand over his heart, where his medal glowed warmly.
⚔️ Sir James: I promise to always be brave, kind, honest, and true. I promise to help others and never give up on my dreams.
📖 Narrator: Claude stood beside him and barked once, as if making the same promise. They were a team, now and forever.
